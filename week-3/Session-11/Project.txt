Class Project: 
- Design a simple webpage for a fictional online bookstore. 
- The webpage should include a well-structured table displaying book details 
    (title, author, genre, price), an interactive form for users to subscribe 
    to a newsletter (collecting name, email, and preferences), and incorporate
     various HTML5 semantic tags to ensure accessibility and readability.
- Additionally, embed an image banner and a promotional video on the page.
